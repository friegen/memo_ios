## 説明文（1506 / 4,000 文字）
```
Irodori Notes is a simple notepad that lets you add color to your text and organize notes in color-coded folders. "Irodori" means "adding color" in Japanese.
It always shows how many characters you have written, so it is handy for drafting essays, reports, social media posts, and applications.

■ Add color to your notes
• 10 text colors, plus bold, italic, and underline
• Highlight what matters so your notes are easy to read at a glance

■ See your character count
• The character and line count of each note is always shown (line breaks are not counted, and each emoji counts as one character)
• Select text to see how many characters it has
• Up to 100,000 characters per note

■ Organize with color-coded folders
• Choose from 10 colors for each folder
• Create folders inside folders (up to 5 levels)
• Sort by last updated, created, characters, name, and more
• Pin your favorite notes and folders to the top

■ Lock important notes
• Open locked notes and folders with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode
• While a locked note is open, its contents are hidden from the app switcher

■ Export and share
• Export notes as PDF, image (PNG), text, or formatted text (HTML) and share them right away

■ And more
• Trash: restore notes within 30 days
• Dark mode, adjustable text size and line spacing
• Included in iCloud Backup, so your notes move with you to a new iPhone

■ Privacy
Your notes are stored on your device and are never sent to the developer's servers.

This app is free and shows ads.
```
